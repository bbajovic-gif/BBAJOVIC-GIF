MOD2

Normalize, Sort & Assign Codes

Turn inconsistent names into a predictable physical storage system.

MOD2 applies reusable artist-sorting rules, handles exceptions, and assigns local copy numbers that can become practical SKU and storage codes. The result is a master collection that sorts correctly on screen and can also be found quickly on the shelf.

  • Normalize individual artists, bands, classical names and special cases
  • Save reusable sorting rules and apply them to selected records
  • Flag new artists, conflicts and records requiring review
  • Assign local copy numbers, SKU references and storage identifiers
