The workflow

One workflow. Three stages. Ready to sell.

Six connected modules take your collection from imported records to organized stock, ready-to-post listings and a storefront for selling.

  1. 01

    Import your records

    Load a Discogs CSV export or connect through the Discogs API. Review differences before updating your master collection.

    Import & compare →
  2. 02

    Organize your stock for sale

    MOD2 · Organize: clean names and formats; assign physical copy codes.
    MOD3 · Enrich: add release details and cover images.
    MOD4 · Manage: set prices, track availability and record sales.

    Collection & stock tools →
  3. 03

    Prepare, publish and sell

    MOD5: prepare marketplace drafts and use assisted posting.
    MOD6: build catalogs and a storefront to showcase your stock for sale.

    MOD5 · List on marketplaces ↗Prepare drafts and reuse listings with your own platform templates, HTML where supported and emoji.MOD6 · Publish your storefront ↗Choose a layout, generate and preview your catalog or website files, then publish your stock for buyers.

↻ Sold a record or changed a price? Update your inventory, then regenerate the output you need.

What to do at each step

A practical checklist

1. Bring the collection in

  • Load your Discogs CSV or connect through the API.
  • Compare incoming records with your master collection.
  • Review changes and apply the updates you approve.
MOD1 · Import & Reconcile →

2. Organize, enrich and manage stock for sale

  • Standardize names and formats; assign physical copy codes.
  • Add release information and cover images.
  • Review prices, availability and sold records.
MOD2 · Organize →MOD3 · Enrich →MOD4 · Manage stock →

3. Prepare listings, publish and sell

  • For marketplaces: research prices, prepare ready-to-post drafts and reuse them with your own platform templates, HTML where supported and emoji.
  • For a catalog or website: choose a layout, generate files and check the preview before publishing.
  • After inventory changes, regenerate the relevant output.
MOD5 · Prepare listings →MOD6 · Build catalogs & websites →
