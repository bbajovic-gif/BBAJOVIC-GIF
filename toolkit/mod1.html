MOD1

Import & Reconcile

Build a reliable master collection from the very first step.

MOD1 imports your Discogs collection either from a downloaded Discogs CSV export or directly through the Discogs API. It compares that source with the local master collection, identifies differences, and lets you decide exactly what should change.

  • Import a downloaded Discogs collection CSV
  • Load collection data directly through the Discogs API
  • Detect new, changed, missing and review-required records
  • Apply controlled repairs without overwriting trusted local data
Downloaded CSVLoad the collection export downloaded from your Discogs account.
Direct Discogs APIConnect and retrieve the latest collection data without manually opening the CSV.
Side-by-side comparisonMaster and Discogs data remain visible together for quick verification.
Issues panelSee clean, changed and review-required records at a glance.
Controlled save & syncRepair selected fields, save the master and synchronize only when ready.

Two ways to begin

Choose the import path that fits your workflow.

Both methods feed the same reconciliation engine and produce the same controlled comparison against your master collection.

Direct Discogs API

Connect to the Discogs account and retrieve current collection information directly.

  • Avoids downloading a new CSV for every comparison
  • Uses the latest data available from the account
  • Feeds directly into the same review and repair workflow
