MOD4

Inventory & Sales Cockpit

The operational heart of the entire Discogs Toolkit.

MOD4 turns the enriched master collection into a working seller system. Records move from Collection to Inventory, are prepared and synchronized with Discogs, and finally move into a persistent Sold ledger. Flexible master views expose exactly the columns needed for pricing, valuation, marketplace work, reports, analytics or diagnostics.

  • Promote records from Collection to sale-ready Inventory
  • Prepare, validate and synchronize Discogs listings
  • Move sold items into a permanent sales ledger
  • Switch between Collection, Inventory and Sold workspaces
  • Build flexible views from the complete master dataset
