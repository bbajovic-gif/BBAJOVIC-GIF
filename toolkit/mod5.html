MOD5

Market Research & Assisted Posting

The dedicated seller workspace for knowing what a record is worth — and getting it listed.

MOD5 combines detailed marketplace research with practical listing preparation. Search comparable offers, compare asking prices, keep Discogs Suggested visible as the master pricing reference, and turn the findings into a reusable research report. The same prepared record can then move into assisted posting packages for multiple seller platforms.

Research

Inspect comparable offers instead of guessing.

MOD5 lets a seller review actual marketplace results around the selected artist, title and release. The workspace keeps source, price, condition, location and listing access together so weak comparisons can be rejected and relevant ones can guide the decision.
