A MAP FOR THE HUNT

88 places. Your kind of finds.

85 thrift shops and 3 optional commercial music stores, from Barrie through the GTA to Burlington. Independent charity shops are a curated selection.

Photos & verification

Photos from official branch pages are included for 10 stores. Other previews show a clearly labelled chain card, not a fabricated storefront. Each place links to its source and identifies what is known about CDs and vinyl.

Locations were researched November 5, 2026. This map was built November 7. Listings and inventory can change. The Barrie Salvation Army relocation has conflicting dates: call before visiting.

Plan a trip & explore nearby

Enter a start and up to three destinations. Review matched addresses, then calculate a lower-distance driving order with optional return to start. Zoom around a trip stop and pick stores on the map, up to eight destinations in total, then update the route. Longer routes also include smaller Google Maps sections for phones. Estimates use OSRM road data, without live traffic, fuel prices, toll costs or store opening hours. Nearby filters show stores within 10 or 15 km as the crow flies. Address queries go to Esri, and route coordinates go to OSRM; trip addresses are not saved unless you explicitly save Home in this browser.
