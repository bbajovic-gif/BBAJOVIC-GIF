The story behind BBaya.net

Collector first.
Builder by necessity.

Sixty years of collecting music, a lifelong love of computers, and one retirement project that grew into a connected system for organizing, presenting and selling media.

BBaya began as a practical way to manage a large personal collection. As the collection grew, so did the need for better research, clearer presentation, consistent visual assets, useful catalogs and less repetitive selling work.

Discogs Toolkit, RDM Play Studio and IconLab developed from those real needs. The website is therefore not only a place where the collection is shown—it is also a working demonstration of the tools behind it.

Everything you see is connected: the collection provides the purpose, the software provides the tools, and the design brings them together.
